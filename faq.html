    Kindness

    cruelty free, vegan, feathers

    p. 04

    Q.Is cruelty free the same thing as vegan?

    asked by confused in Turin

    A.

    Not quite. Cruelty free means nothing was tested on animals, at any step, by us or our suppliers. Vegan means nothing in the formula comes from an animal. We are both, for every product and every shade, always.

    Q.Are all those wings and feathers real?

    asked by bird lover, Bologna

    A.

    Never. Every feather you see is an illustration or a prop made of synthetic fibre. No bird was ever involved, not even the pigeon who wandered onto our set.

    Q.How are your pinks so pink without carmine?

    asked by rosy cheeks, Naples

    A.

    Iron oxides, plant pigments like beetroot and skin-safe synthetic colours. Just as rosy, and no insects are crushed. You can check any ingredient yourself with our ingredient checker.

    Q.Do you sell anywhere that requires animal testing?

    asked by just checking, Vienna

    A.

    No. If a market requires animal testing for cosmetics, we simply don’t sell there. Read the whole promise in our vow.

    02

    Products

    shades, skin, lashes & brushes

    p. 06

    Q.How do I find my shade online?

    asked by pale & panicking, Milan

    A.

    Tell your Angel club card your undertone and favourite finish and it will choose shades for you. You can also search by colour. Remember that screens tint colours a little, so look at the swatch in daylight.

    Q.My skin is very sensitive. Can I wear your makeup?

    asked by easily blushed, Rome

    A.

    Our formulas are fragrance-light and every pack lists its full ingredients. Still, every skin is different: try a little on your inner arm for 24 hours first, and ask your dermatologist if you have a condition.

    Q.How many times can I wear Fallen Lash?

    asked by fluttering, Florence

    A.

    Up to 20 wears. Peel the glue off gently after each use, clean them with a cotton bud and micellar water, and keep them in their case so they stay curled. They’re synthetic: never mink, silk or feathers.

    Q.How do I clean my Wing Brush?

    asked by messy angel, Genoa

    A.

    Once a week, swirl it in lukewarm water with a drop of gentle soap, rinse until the water runs clear, reshape it and let it dry lying flat. The synthetic bristles dry fast and never shed.

    Q.How long does my makeup last once opened?

    asked by hoarder, Palermo

    A.

    Look for the little open-jar symbol on the pack: “12M” means twelve months after opening. As a rule, liners and mascaras last 6 months, lips and liquids 12, and powders like Cloud Veil and Pearl Palette up to 24.

    03

    Orders

    checkout, gifts & codes

    p. 09

    Q.Why can’t I check out yet?

    asked by impatient, Verona

    A.

    Our shop is still in preview, so you can fill your bag and play with it, but no payment is taken and no order is sent. Checkout opens soon. Write to us and we’ll tell you first.

    Q.Can you gift-wrap my order?

    asked by a secret admirer

    A.

    Of course. Tick “wrap it in a satin bow” in your bag (+ € 3) and we’ll wrap everything in tissue with a satin-effect ribbon, made of recycled polyester, never silk. Prices are never printed inside a gift.

    Q.Where do I type a promo code?

    asked by bargain angel, Bari

    A.

    In your bag, right under the gift wrap. One code per order, and codes can’t be swapped for cash. The codes that work in the preview are just for trying things out.

    04

    Shipping & returns

    parcels, timing, changing your mind

    p. 11

    Q.How much is shipping, and when is it free?

    asked by counting coins, Trieste

    A.

    In Italy it’s € 4,90, free from € 40. Every other country has its own rate and free-shipping line. Try the little calculator on our shipping page.

    Q.How long will my parcel take to fly to me?

    asked by waiting by the window

    A.

    We pack in 1–2 working days, then it’s 2–4 working days in Italy and 4–7 across the EU. You’ll get a tracking link by email the moment it leaves. Follow the whole journey.

    Q.I changed my mind. Can I send it back?

    asked by fickle heart, Padua

    A.

    Yes, within 30 days, as long as it’s unopened and still sealed. For hygiene we can’t take back makeup that has been used, unless it arrived faulty. How returns work.

    Q.My compact arrived broken!

    asked by heartbroken, Cagliari

    A.

    We’re so sorry. Take a photo and write to us within 14 days, and we’ll send a new one for free. You don’t need to send the broken one back.

    05

    Account

    the Angel club & your data

    p. 14

    Q.Do I need an account to shop?

    asked by free spirit, Lecce

    A.

    No. The Angel club is just a cosy place for your wishlist, tarot card and vow. Right now it only needs a name, never a password, and it lives on your device.

    Q.What do you know about me?

    asked by private person, Parma

    A.

    For now, nothing leaves your browser. Your bag, hearts and card are kept on your own device, and you can see and erase every single thing on our privacy page.
