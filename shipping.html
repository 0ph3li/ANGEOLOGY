    Call it off?

    You have 30 days to send it back.

    1. i.

      Write to us

      Within 30 days of delivery, send us a letter with your order number and what you’d like to return.

    2. ii.

      Send it back

      We send you the return address and, in Italy, a free prepaid label. From other countries the return postage is yours.

    3. iii.

      Get your refund

      Within 14 days of the parcel reaching us, we refund you on the payment method you used.

    • Unopened & sealed products can come back for any reason.
    • Used makeup can’t come back, for everyone’s hygiene, unless it was faulty.
    • Broken or wrong? Send a photo within 14 days and we replace it for free. No need to return it.
    • Your legal rights under EU consumer law always apply. Our 30 days are simply longer than the 14 required.
