    Our story

    a diary, kept since the very first gloss

    dear diary,

    today I refused to put beeswax in my lip gloss.

    tomorrow I’ll refuse to test it on anything that can’t say yes.

    one day, a whole makeup house will refuse with me.

    — her, MMXXV

    1. 01 · 02 · 2025

      The first gloss

      A saucepan, a sunflower wax and a lot of pink. The very first Halo Gloss was stirred on a kitchen table — beeswax was never on the list.

    2. 14 · 05 · 2025

      The vow

      Written on a café napkin: never tested on animals, never made from them. It still lives, framed, above the studio door.

      read the vow

    3. 30 · 09 · 2025

      The name

      Angeology — the study of angels. Because every girl deserves a halo and a sharp wing. Our very first muse tried the name on like a lip colour, and it fit.

    4. 12 · 01 · 2026

      The first box

      Two hundred orders, two hundred satin bows, tied by hand on the living-room floor. Our fingers still remember.

      see what’s inside now

    5. today

      Still kind

      Sixteen vegan formulas, four collections, one lookbook — and still zero animals harmed. The diary keeps going.

    a letter from our founder

    Dear angel,

    I grew up loving pink things and loud eyeliner, and hating that so much of the makeup I loved had hurt someone small to exist. So I started making my own.

    Angeology is for the girls who wear lace and bullet belts on the same day. Soft hearts, sharp edges — and a promise that nothing in your makeup bag ever cost an animal anything.

    Thank you for choosing kind. It’s the prettiest thing you’ll wear.

    with love & glitter,

    — the founder

    04

    What we believe

    • Kind, always

      Never tested on animals, never made from them. Not a trend — the whole point.

    • Soft & sharp

      Coquette and alternative aren’t opposites. Wear the bow and the bullet belt.

    • Every doll, every shade

      Our skin tints and brows come in shades for every angel — and we keep adding more.

    • Less, but prettier

      Recycled card, refillable compacts and bows you’ll want to keep in your hair.
