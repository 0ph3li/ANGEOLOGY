    Your secrets

    are safe with us

    What we know about you, what we don’t, and how to make this site forget you in one tap. Every section has a pink note that says it in plain words.

    privacy policylast updated 28 September 2026read the terms

    draft Written for the preview of the site. Details in [brackets] are filled in, and the page is reviewed by a legal professional, before the shop opens.

    on this page

      The short version

      Right now this website doesn’t send anything about you to us. There are no accounts on a server, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies. The things you save (your bag, hearts, tarot card, vow and Angel club card) stay in your own browser, and you can erase them below.

      in short

      We don’t collect anything yet. Your choices stay on your device.

      What this device remembers

      These are the things this site keeps in your browser’s local storage, live from this device. They never leave it, and they disappear if you clear your browser data or tap “forget”.

      What we never do

      We don’t sell or rent your data. We don’t build advertising profiles. We don’t use tracking pixels, fingerprinting or third-party cookies. If that ever changed, we would ask you first. We won’t simply update this page.

      in short

      No selling, no tracking, no ads following you around.

      Fonts & scripts from other servers

      To show our typefaces and animations, your browser downloads files from Google Fonts (Google) and cdnjs (Cloudflare). Like any website you visit, these servers receive your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the files. You can read Google’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Before launch we plan to host these files ourselves.

      in short

      Two services deliver our fonts and scripts, and they see your IP address.

      Letters you write to us

      The contact form is not connected yet, so nothing you type there is sent. When it is, we’ll use your name, email and message only to reply to you, keep them for up to 24 months, and never add you to a mailing list without asking.

      in short

      Your letters will only be used to write back.

      When the shop opens

      To deliver an order we’ll need your name, email, shipping address and, if you choose, a phone number for the courier. We use this to fulfil the contract with you. Invoices are kept for 10 years because Italian tax law requires it.

      Payments go directly to [payment provider] and deliveries to [courier]. We share with them only what they need. We never see or store your full card number.

      in short

      Only what’s needed to get your parcel to you, and nothing more.

      Your rights

      Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your data, restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw any consent you gave. Just write to us and we’ll answer within a month.

      You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority in your own country.

      in short

      Your data, your say. Ask us anything, any time.

      Little angels

      Our shop is meant for people aged 14 and over. If you’re younger, please ask a grown-up to order for you.

      in short

      14 and up, please.

      Who looks after your data

      The data controller is [ANGEOLOGY S.r.l.], [registered address], Italy. Write to hello@angeology.com or use our contact page. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

      in short

      That’s us. Say hi.
